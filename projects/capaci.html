Product Launch

Capaci

The Challenge

Most productivity tools assume you have infinite capacity. They're built around what you need to do, not whether you actually have the energy, focus, or mental space to do it. That's why most people's task lists become a source of stress instead of clarity.

What I Did

I started building Capaci from a real problem I was experiencing. The concept is capacity-aware planning, which means the app thinks about your actual load instead of just stacking work on top of work.

I defined the product experience, the check-in system, the capacity logic, the UI, and the feature requirements. I'm using AI-assisted development to build and iterate, partly because it's efficient and partly because building a product about realistic capacity means understanding my own constraints as I go.

Outcome

Capaci is a functioning early version that's actively being developed and refined. It's also a case study in how I work: taking an ambiguous problem from lived experience, defining it clearly, designing around how people actually behave, then building something that works.
