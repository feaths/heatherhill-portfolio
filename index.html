About Heather

I'm Heather Hill, a digital product and marketing consultant who has spent twelve years moving between customer operations, engineering, team leadership, and product work. That path was not a plan. It turned out to be the point. It taught me to see the connections between the technical, operational, and human sides of a problem that most specialists only see one piece of.

Right now, I'm focused on helping small businesses and founders who need someone who can think structurally and still get their hands dirty, on the website, the copy, and the systems behind it.
